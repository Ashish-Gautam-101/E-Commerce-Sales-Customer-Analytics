# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset to uncover insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and business trends.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow using MySQL, Python, and Power BI, from data validation and cleaning to SQL analysis and dashboard visualization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives


Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
Identify top-performing products and categories
Understand customer purchasing behavior
Analyze monthly sales trends
Identify high-value and repeat customers
Evaluate seller performance
Analyze order and payment patterns
Generate actionable business insights
---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses the **Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist**.

The dataset contains information related to:

- Customers
- Orders
- Order Items
- Products
- Sellers
- Payments
- Reviews
- Geolocation
- Product Category Translation

# 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the Power BI dashboards:

1. The overall dashboard reports **16.01M total revenue** from approximately **99K orders** and **96K customers**.
2. The overall **Average Order Value is 160.99**.
3. The overall **Average Review Score is 4.09**, indicating a strong average customer rating in the displayed data.
4. There are approximately **3K repeat customers**, representing a **3.1% repeat customer rate**.
5. **28K customers** are classified as premium customers in the dashboard.
6. **São Paulo (SP)** is the highest-revenue customer state among the states displayed in the customer analysis.
7. The Executive Dashboard shows **credit card** as the dominant payment method by revenue, contributing approximately **78.34%** of the displayed payment revenue.
8. Product analysis reports **13.59M total product sales** across approximately **113K products**.
9. The Product Analysis dashboard highlights `beleza_saude`, `relogios_presentes`, and `cama_mesa_banho` among the leading displayed product categories.
10. Seller analysis provides seller-level revenue, order count, and average order value to compare seller performance.

> These insights are based on the values and visuals displayed in the Power BI report.
